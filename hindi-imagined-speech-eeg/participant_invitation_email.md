# Participant Invitation Email

**Subject:** Invitation to Participate in Hindi Imagined Speech EEG Study at Mahindra University

Dear Student,

We are inviting healthy adult Hindi-speaking volunteers aged **18–35 years** to participate in a research study being conducted by the Department of AI & DS at Mahindra University.

### About the study

The study records **EEG (electroencephalography)** while participants silently imagine saying Hindi words. The purpose is to develop a research dataset for Hindi imagined-speech / brain-computer interface research.

During the session, you will:

- receive a short explanation and complete the consent and screening process;
- wear a non-invasive EEG headset;
- see Hindi words on a screen;
- silently imagine saying each word without speaking or moving your lips; and
- take scheduled breaks between recording blocks.

The current protocol includes a practice stage and four main recording blocks. The study is non-invasive and is considered minimal risk; possible temporary discomforts include mild headset pressure and mental fatigue.

Participation is completely voluntary. You may pause or stop the session at any time without penalty.
