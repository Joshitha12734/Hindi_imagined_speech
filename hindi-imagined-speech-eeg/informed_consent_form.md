## Participant statement

Please read each statement carefully. The researcher will explain the study to you in a language you understand and will answer your questions before you decide whether to participate.

### 1. Understanding of the study

I confirm that I have read and/or had explained to me the **Participant Information Sheet** for the Hindi Imagined Speech EEG Study. I have had enough opportunity to ask questions, and my questions have been answered satisfactorily.

- [ ] I understand the purpose of the study.
- [ ] I understand that this is research and not a medical diagnosis, treatment, or mental-health assessment.
- [ ] I understand that the study records EEG signals while I silently imagine saying Hindi words.

### 2. What I will be asked to do

I understand that:

- I will be seated in front of a screen while EEG is recorded.
- Hindi words will be displayed during the experiment.
- I will silently imagine saying each displayed word in my own voice.
- I should not speak, whisper, lip-sync, or intentionally move my lips or tongue during the imagined-speech period.
- The current trial sequence is **500 ms fixation → 300 ms word display → 3,000 ms imagined speech → 1,000 ms rest/reset**.
- The current protocol includes 6 randomized Hindi word classes/stimuli.
- The session includes a practice stage and four main recording blocks, with rest breaks between blocks.

### 3. EEG recording

I understand that EEG is a non-invasive recording method using sensors placed on the scalp. I understand that the EEG system records brain-related electrical signals and **does not electrically stimulate my brain**.

I understand that the final EEG hardware configuration is subject to the study team's confirmed protocol.

### 4. Possible discomforts and risks

I understand that possible temporary discomforts may include mild headset pressure, scalp dryness or stickiness, and mental fatigue from concentrating during the task.

I understand that:

- the equipment can be adjusted if it is uncomfortable;
- breaks are provided;
- I can ask for the headset to be removed immediately if I feel uncomfortable; and
- I can pause or stop participation at any time without penalty.

### 5. Voluntary participation and withdrawal

I understand that participation is voluntary. I am free to refuse to participate or to stop participating at any time, without penalty and without effect on my academic standing or relationship with Mahindra University.

I understand that I may ask questions before, during, or after the session.

### 6. Confidentiality and coding

I understand that I will be assigned a study ID and that my name and direct identifying information will be kept separately from my EEG research data.

I understand that authorized members of the research team may access the coded research data for approved study purposes.

I understand that the research data will be stored on secure institutional systems and that consent documents will be stored separately.

### 7. Research publication

I understand that the research team may publish scientific findings from this study in academic journals, conferences, reports, or other appropriate research outputs.

I understand that publications and shared datasets will not intentionally include my direct personal identifiers.

### 8. Future use and data sharing

I understand that, subject to my consent and institutional policy, **anonymized EEG data** collected in this study may be:

- used in future brain-computer interface classification research; and/or
- shared as an open-access research dataset for legitimate scientific research.

I understand that direct personal identifiers will not be included in the released anonymized dataset.

### 9. Withdrawal and deletion of data

I understand that I may request withdrawal from the study.

I understand that deletion of my data can be requested while the data remain identifiable or reversibly coded, subject to the study's approved data-management procedure.

I understand that once data have been irreversibly anonymized and publicly released, it may no longer be possible to identify or remove my data from copies that have already been distributed.

### 10. Data retention

I understand that the study materials specify a **minimum retention period of five years**, subject to institutional policy and the approved study requirements.

### 11. Benefits

I understand that I may not receive a direct personal benefit from participating. I understand that the expected benefit is primarily to scientific research and future Hindi-language brain-computer interface development.

### 12. Compensation / reimbursement

I understand that any reimbursement, compensation, or other participant payment will be provided only if it is specified in the final ethics-approved participant materials.

### 13. Research results

I understand that the study is intended for dataset development and proof-of-concept research. I understand that individual EEG recordings are not intended to provide a diagnosis or clinical interpretation.

### 14. Questions or concerns

I understand that I may contact the study team if I have questions about the study or my participation.
