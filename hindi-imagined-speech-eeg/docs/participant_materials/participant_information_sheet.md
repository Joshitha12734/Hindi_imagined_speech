## 1. Why are you being invited?

You are being invited to take part in a research study that records brain activity using electroencephalography (EEG) while you silently imagine saying Hindi words.

The purpose is to develop a research dataset for Hindi imagined-speech / brain-computer interface (BCI) research. The study is for research and dataset development. It is **not a medical test, diagnosis, treatment, or mental-health assessment**.

## 2. Why is this study being done?

Many imagined-speech BCI datasets have focused on languages such as English and other languages. This study aims to develop a structured Hindi EEG resource that can support future research on Hindi-language BCI systems and proof-of-concept classification of imagined-word conditions and rest.

## 3. What will happen if you participate?

If you agree to participate:

1. A researcher will explain the study and answer your questions.
2. You will complete the approved participant screening procedure.
3. You will provide written informed consent before recording.
4. An EEG headset will be fitted on your scalp and the signal quality will be checked.
5. If the eye-tracking component is retained in the final setup, an eye-tracker calibration may be performed.
6. You will receive instructions and complete a practice block.
7. During recording, Hindi words will appear on a screen. You will silently imagine saying each displayed word in your own voice.
8. You will **not** be asked to speak aloud, press a button, or deliberately move your lips during the imagined-speech period.
9. You will have rest periods between recording blocks and may ask for a pause at any time.
10. At the end, the headset will be removed and you may ask questions or provide feedback.

## 4. What does one trial look like?

The current protocol specifies the following sequence:

| Stage | Duration | What you do |
|---|---:|---|
| Fixation | 500 ms | Look at the centre marker and remain still. |
| Word display | 300 ms | Read the Hindi word shown in Devanagari. |
| Imagined speech | 3,000 ms | Silently imagine saying the word in your own voice. |
| Rest / reset | 1,000 ms | Relax before the next randomized trial. |

The current recording protocol uses **6 randomized Hindi word classes/stimuli**.

## 5. How many trials are there?

The current protocol specifies:

- Practice: **55 trials (~4 minutes)**
- Main block 1: **110 trials (~8.8 minutes)**
- Main block 2: **110 trials (~8.8 minutes)**
- Main block 3: **110 trials (~8.8 minutes)**
- Main block 4: **110 trials (~8.8 minutes)**

Scheduled rest breaks occur between blocks. The exact break duration remains subject to the final approved session plan.

## 6. What is EEG?

EEG records electrical activity associated with brain activity using electrodes placed on the scalp. The study is non-invasive. The EEG system does **not** apply electrical stimulation to your brain.

The final EEG hardware is still subject to confirmation in the study protocol. Device-specific details will be explained before recording.

## 7. Are there any risks or discomforts?

The study is considered minimal risk in the current ethics materials. Possible minor discomforts include:

- Mild pressure from the EEG headset.
- Temporary scalp dryness or stickiness depending on the equipment used.
- Mental fatigue from concentrating on the task for an extended period.
- Mild discomfort associated with wearing headgear.

The equipment can be adjusted, breaks are provided, and the headset can be removed immediately if you are uncomfortable.

You may pause or stop participation at any time without penalty.

## 8. Are there any benefits?

You may not receive a direct personal benefit from participating. The expected benefit is to research: your coded EEG contribution may help develop a Hindi-language dataset for future BCI and imagined-speech research.

The study does not promise that an individual's imagined words can be decoded successfully.

## 9. Is participation voluntary?

Yes. Participation is entirely voluntary.

You may:

- Ask questions before, during, or after the session.
- Pause or stop the session at any time.
- Withdraw without penalty or effect on your academic standing.

If your data are still identifiable or reversibly coded, you may request deletion according to the approved study procedure. Once data have been irreversibly anonymized and publicly released, it may no longer be possible to identify and remove your individual contribution.

## 10. What happens to your information?

You will be assigned a study ID. Your name and direct identifying information will be kept separate from EEG research data.

Research data will be stored on secure institutional systems with access limited to authorized members of the research team. Consent forms will be stored separately from the EEG dataset.

The research dataset is intended to contain coded/anonymized research information rather than names or direct identifiers.

## 11. Will the data be shared?

The study may publish research findings. Subject to your consent, institutional policy, and applicable approvals, anonymized EEG data may be used in future BCI classification research and may be released as an open research dataset.

The published dataset will not contain direct personal identifiers.

## 12. How long will the data be kept?

The current ethics presentation states a **minimum five-year retention period**. Final retention and future-use arrangements will follow the approved institutional policy and consent form.
