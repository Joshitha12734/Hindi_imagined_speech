# Participant Instructions

**Study:** Hindi Imagined Speech EEG Study  
**Protocol version:** 1.0

## Before we begin

1. Sit comfortably and listen carefully to the researcher's instructions.
2. Ask any questions you have before recording starts.
3. Tell the researcher immediately if the EEG equipment feels uncomfortable.
4. During recording, try to remain relaxed and minimize unnecessary movement.

## Your task

You will see Hindi words on the screen.

Your job is to **silently imagine saying the Hindi word in your own voice**.

You do **not** need to say the word aloud.

### Please do

- Look at the screen as instructed.
- Read the Hindi word when it appears.
- Silently imagine saying the word.
- Imagine the word clearly in your own voice.
- Stay as still and relaxed as reasonably possible.
- Relax during the rest/reset period.

### Please do not

- Do not speak aloud.
- Do not whisper.
- Do not mouth or lip-sync the word.
- Do not intentionally move your lips or tongue to produce the word.
- Do not press a button to indicate your answer unless the researcher specifically instructs you to do so.
- Do not worry about getting a "correct" EEG signal on any individual trial.

## What happens in one trial?

```text
500 ms
FIXATION
Look at the centre marker
        ↓
300 ms
WORD DISPLAY
Read the Hindi word
        ↓
3,000 ms
IMAGINED SPEECH
Silently imagine saying the word
        ↓
1,000 ms
REST / RESET
Relax before the next trial
```

The Hindi word disappears before the imagined-speech period, so you should rely on your memory of the word during that period.

## Practice

Before the main recording, the researcher will demonstrate the task and you will complete a practice block.

The current protocol specifies **55 practice trials (~4 minutes)**.

## Main recording

The current protocol specifies four main blocks:

| Block | Trials | Approx. duration |
|---|---:|---:|
| Main Block 1 | 110 | ~8.8 min |
| Main Block 2 | 110 | ~8.8 min |
| Main Block 3 | 110 | ~8.8 min |
| Main Block 4 | 110 | ~8.8 min |

You will receive scheduled rest breaks between blocks.

## If you feel uncomfortable

You can ask for a break or ask the researcher to stop the recording at any time.

There is no penalty for stopping.

If the EEG headset becomes uncomfortable, tell the researcher immediately so it can be adjusted or removed.

## Most important instruction

> **When a Hindi word appears, read it and then silently imagine saying that word in your own voice. Do not say it aloud or move your mouth to say it.**

If you are unsure about what to do during the experiment, ask the researcher before continuing.
